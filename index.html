About Us

KBT College of Engineering was established with the vision of providing world-class technical education. The college is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University and offers AICTE-approved undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs.

We focus on academic excellence, research, innovation, personality development, and industry-oriented training. Our campus provides smart classrooms, advanced laboratories, a central library, sports facilities, hostels, and placement support for students.
